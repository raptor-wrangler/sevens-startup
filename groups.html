    Groups

    Find others who enjoy the same games as you!

    When we get to the Javascript part of this project, I will make it so that when someone favorites a game, they are added to a list of people who like said game. On this page, they will be able to see a table populated will all the people who also like their games. From here, they can email the other person or multiple people in group chains. There will need to be a way to filter the list to only show the people who like 1 specific game at a time.

    This is also where websocket will come into play. If another user likes a game you do, you will be notified of that.
