    About

    My name is Seven, a computer Science: Human Computer Interaction major at BYU. This website was made for a web programming class. It will hopefully be just one of many!

    Connect 4 Gaming is a website created in order to help people who like to play board games find and meet other players in person. I am a board game lover myself and it's difficult to find people who are willing to play the more complicated games that I love.
